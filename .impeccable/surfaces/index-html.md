# Holly Jones SFW website

- Surface: brand
- Product lane: premium web presence for content creators and local businesses — Designs of Desire studio. Never use a generic SaaS or corporate template look.
- Scope: preserve this established SFW site and its voice while keeping its outbound destinations to OnlyFans, LoyalFans, and Throne. Analytics should group clicks by destination, independent of placement.
- Visual direction: retain the existing client-specific visual identity for scoped maintenance. Make future palette and typography decisions from Holly's brief and assets; do not reuse a fixed palette across clients.
- Anti-references: cream background with a heavy serif headline and terracotta/rust accent; near-black background with a single neon accent; centered hero with gradient blob and pill buttons; Inter, Poppins, or Roboto for both display and body; repeated three-card feature grids; arbitrary 01/02/03 numbering; generic stock photography; star-rating testimonial carousels; four-column footer on a one-page site.
