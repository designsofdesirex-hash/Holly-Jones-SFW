# Holly Jones color palettes

Both Holly Jones sites offer selectable color systems. Each includes its own hue family and accent, and selections persist independently per site. The original opens in Velvet Violet; the SFW site opens in Noir & Gilt. The SFW site also restores its earlier multicolor editorial look as an additional option, listed first as Heritage.

## Noir & Gilt

Black `#090909`, porcelain white `#F8F6F0`, and antique gold `#B09152`.

## Velvet Violet

Deep aubergine `#211936`, orchid `#D95A9A`, and pale lilac `#F3EAF7`.

## Pacific Tide

Ocean blue `#083D52`, warm coral `#F0785D`, and clear aqua `#E1F3F5`.

## Citrus Grove

Botanical green `#264E36`, bright citron `#C5DF59`, and soft citrus cream `#F3F4D9`.

## Heritage (SFW)

The earlier editorial mix of aubergine, forest, burgundy, blush, sage, and deep teal section gradients, with the original cream, charcoal, and antique-gold brand colors.
